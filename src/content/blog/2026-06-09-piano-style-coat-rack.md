I saw a piano-style coat rack and wanted to build my own version. The idea is simple: a row of "keys" in alternating walnut and maple, where the sharps and flats pivot down to become hooks.

## The plan

- Walnut for the dark keys, maple for the naturals.
- A French cleat on the back for mounting.
- Brass pins as the pivot points.

## First decisions

The biggest open question is the pivot mechanism. Brass rod through the full width keeps everything aligned, but individual pins make repairs easier. I'm leaning toward a single rod.

More to come once the lumber is milled.
