The radial arm saw has been living on a pair of sawhorses for too long. Time for a proper station.

## Requirements

- Long infeed and outfeed wings for breaking down 8-foot stock.
- A replaceable sacrificial fence and table surface.
- A dust hood behind the blade — radial arm saws throw chips *everywhere*.
- Storage underneath for blades and jigs.

## Dimensions

Working height will match the planer cart at 34 inches so the two can share outfeed duties. Total length around 10 feet along the shop's north wall.

Next step: sketch the cabinet bases and figure out the material list.
