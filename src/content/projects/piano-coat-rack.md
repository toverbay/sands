A wall-mounted coat rack inspired by piano keys. Alternating walnut and maple keys, with the accidentals pivoting down on brass pins to act as hooks.

## Build notes

- Mill walnut and maple to matching thickness.
- Cut keys to length on a crosscut sled.
- Drill pivot holes with a doweling jig for alignment.
- Finish with Danish oil before assembly.
